## License notes

- CC BY and CC BY-SA require attribution (author, license, link). The About screen
  carries all three. Attribution off-post is standard practice for these licenses.
- CC BY-SA: the resized photos are adaptations and stay under their BY-SA license.
  That covers the image files only, not the game's code.
- Picked against: AI-generated "stock" uploads on Commons (several 2026 uploads look
  generated). Real photographs only.
