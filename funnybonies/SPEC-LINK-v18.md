# Funnybonies v18 - spec link (the sandbox)

Tight Spiral Studios · 2026-11-09 · Status: LAB. Player: kids, grades 3 to 6. Supersedes v17 (Wake Bloat) as the playable direction. v16 and v17 stay in the folder as the art base.

## Founder words, 2026-11-09 (verbatim)

> "None of the funnybones builds make me laugh. Besides bloats original supersize. Make all the Humor in the playground be discoverable and intuitive and genuine. This game should work like a Sandbox"

> "Look, don't build around bloat honestly."

## What changed and why

| v15 to v17 did this | v18 does this instead |
|---|---|
| Captions explained each joke as it happened ("That is exaggeration.") | No caption explains a joke. The Found book names the move after the laugh. |
| One scripted gag per tap, in levels | Every robot x every prop has its own reaction. The player finds them. |
| A laugh meter scored the player | Three Bonies on the wall laugh at what is new to them and yawn at repeats. |
| Bloat was level 1, the spine | Ensemble of four. Mote and Tock start in the yard. Flip cartwheels in, then Bloat. |
| Pump meter, Launch button, hint rings | No meters, no hint rings. One in-character invitation: Mote's bubble says "poke." |

Bloat's original supersize is kept as what it was: his hello when he arrives, and his poke.

## The matrix (the content of the game)

Verbs: poke (tap), fling (drag and let go), peel (put one in someone's path), pie (fling it at a face), bucket (drop it on a head), feather (rub it on someone). 4 x 6 = 24 reactions, plus 3 found by accident: Poke Bloat a lot, Seesaw launch, Midair bonk. 27 entries in the Found book.

| | Bloat: exaggeration | Mote: understatement | Tock: timing | Flip: reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poke | Puffs up enormous | "hm." | Tick, tock, tick... OW | Flips onto his hands, walks upside down |
| Fling | Boing, Boing, BOING, bigger each time | Lands flat, the yard shakes, "hm." | His alarm rings; everyone jumps but Mote | Perfect landing, on his head. A leaf topples him |
| Peel | A dozen spins across the yard | Slides the whole yard standing up | Freezes on one foot, falls on beat three | Backflip, lands it, bows, falls over |
| Pie | Puffs, the pie rockets off | Wipes one eye. "hm." | Nothing, nothing, then all of it | Dodges. Another pie falls from the sky onto him |
| Bucket | Swells until it pops off | Keeps it on, walks into the wall | BONG BONG BONG | Kicks it away, it lands on his head |
| Feather | Giggles himself into a balloon, floats | Long rub: "heh." | Laughs on the beat: ha. ha. ha. ha. | No laugh. A sneeze blows him across the yard |

Reaction shots: when one robot gets got, the others react in their own type (Bloat laughs too big, Mote "hm.", Tock laughs late, Flip says "aww.").

## Learning outcome (never labeled during play)

Each kind of funny has a shape. The Found book names the shape after the player has already laughed at it: Exaggeration, Understatement, Timing, Reversal, Setup and payoff.

## Floors

Single file, offline, stores nothing, no emoji, no green, synthesized sound with a sound toggle, three modes (Light, Dark, Sharp) on one icon button, Studio/Cabinet/Back/Home in the menu, keyboard and voice: every robot and prop is a named button (Enter on a prop, then Enter on a robot).

## Open for the founder

- Arrival pacing (Flip after 3 finds or 45 s, Bloat after 7 finds or 100 s).
- Whether the v17 ride (robots fold into a Rube Goldberg ride) comes back as a later layer once the sandbox is funny on its own.
