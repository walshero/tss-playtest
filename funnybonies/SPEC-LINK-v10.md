# Funnybonies v10 - spec link and re-spine

Tight Spiral Studios · 2026-08-28 · Written before the build.

## Specs this build is made against

1. `studio/laughter-foundry-spec-and-log.html` (The Laughter Foundry, 2026-06-28).
   Founder's own words in that log:
   > *"Create a game that makes kids laugh and shows them that not everyone laughs at
   > the same things. Give them the tools to make pieces for a Rube Goldberg machine of
   > working parts, all designed to induce different types of laughter."*
   The operative phrase v9 skipped is **"a Rube Goldberg machine of working parts."**
   Not a taxonomy. A machine.
2. `rescued/shelf-2026-07-13/funny-boneys-factory-spec.md` (GDD v2) - origin, floors,
   and the standing brief that the look is decided fresh per build.

## Audience

**Kids, roughly grades 3 to 6.** Holding a phone. Very possibly with the sound off.

## Re-spine (founder-called 2026-08-28)

Two rulings, both fatal to v9:

> *"The echo makes no sense. I can't hear anything."*
> *"The game makes no intuitive sense."*

**What each one killed.**

**THE ECHO was a joke made of sound in a game that has none.** A hammer travelled a rail
and struck three bells, and the gag was that the third bell left before it was hit. On a
muted phone that is a stick moving past some shapes. The part is deleted. **New standing
floor: every gag must be fully legible with the sound off, because there is no sound.**
Nothing in this build depends on hearing anything.

**The ordering mechanic was a comedy-theory exam.** v9 asked a kid to choose an ORDER for
three abstractions, then read a paragraph explaining what their choice cost and earned.
Six orders, six verdicts, no machine anywhere. A kid cannot see a chain reaction in a
three-slot dropdown, and the payoff was prose.

## The new spine

**ONE TRACK. ONE BALL. THREE SOCKETS.**

The kid taps parts onto a bench. Presses GO. A ball rolls left to right and each part
does something visibly stupid to it, in order, with real cause and effect between the
beats. The mess it leaves stays on the bench.

**Founder call: machine first, naming after.** The teaching is not removed, it is moved.
Nothing is named until the kid has already watched it happen, and the naming never blocks
the next run.

## The three parts

Every gag is a PICTURE. Each one has anticipation, a pause, and a consequence that stays
on the bench.

| Part | What you see | The line, said afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| **The Hopper** | One ball goes in. A beat of nothing. Fourteen come out. | It does not know when to stop. |
| **The Jack** | Ball goes in. The box wobbles twice. Then a long flat nothing. Then the lid leaves. | The waiting is the joke, not the popping. |
| **The Triplet** | Two identical balls roll out. Pause. The third is absurdly the wrong size. | Two the same makes a rule. The third one breaks it. |

Onion layers, for the record: Hopper is 4 (too much), Jack is 2 (surprise), Triplet is 3
(again but different). **The layer numbers never appear on screen.** They are a build
note, not player-facing vocabulary.

## How it teaches now

1. The kid puts parts on the track for no reason except that they look like something.
2. GO. The machine runs. Something stupid happens to a ball.
3. **Only then** a line appears under the machine naming what they just made happen.
4. It does not block anything. Press GO again, change the parts, run it again.

No score. No wrong answer. No verdict paragraph. The naming is one sentence and a chip
per part used.

## Look

Unchanged from v9 and the only thing that carries: Samorost plus Lumino City, a
miniature that was built and lit. Kraft cardboard, cut paper, one warm work lamp, real
cast shadow. Light theme = shutters open. Dark theme = the work lamp at night.

## Floors (all binding)

No emoji. No green in any structural role. High contrast both themes. 44px targets.
20px type, 18px absolute floor. Reduced motion jumps to the end state and still tells
the outcome. Single file, offline, in memory only. No em dashes. Version chip beside the
title, matching `<title>` and the build banner.

**And the new one: SOUND IS NEVER A CHANNEL.** No gag, cue, or payoff may exist only in
audio. This is now a floor because a founder review found a part that broke it.

## Out of scope

Onion layers 1, 5, 6, 7. More than three parts. Scoring, sharing, stacking, the
real-world-problem layer, the dashboards, any measurement instrument, any ordering puzzle.

## Acceptance

1. A kid can work out what to do without an adult reading anything to them.
2. Every gag is legible with the sound off.
3. Every combination runs. None is punished.
4. The naming arrives after the run and never blocks the next one.
5. The outcome is legible from a screenshot with the text hidden.
