Sandbagsa flash fiction climb

Too heavy to fly. Tap words this piece can live without. Tear the meaning twice, and it crashes.

on the ground
liftoff

The cargo manifest: every word is a sandbag; some are holding the piece up

The heap: weight this flight didn't need (it may fly another day)

nothing dropped yet

It flew
