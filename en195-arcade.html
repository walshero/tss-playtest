How the studio works

How this was made

Built by Tight Spiral Studios with Claude in the loop. The coin economy is a credited lesson from Lure of the Labyrinth (Learning Games Network / MIT Education Arcade, Scot Osterweil): learning embedded in play, earn in one room and spend in another, mystery-first entry. All art is hand-cut SVG made in-file; the coin sound is synthesized in-file with Web Audio; the Line Break text is William Carlos Williams, 1923, public domain, attributed. Everything above the Workshop Vending Machine stores nothing and runs in memory only. The coin ticket is the player's to carry out. The Workshop Vending Machine is the one exception: what you feed it (your certification, group, link, full text, and email) is sent to a server so your workshop can see who has submitted.
