Tight Spiral Productions · MassBay faculty · iSLO 1 & 3

Make the assignment AI-resilient — and say so on the syllabus.

Students are using AI whether or not we've said anything. The fix isn't detection — it's assignment design that rewards the thinking AI can't do for them, plus a clear policy so no one has to guess. Run your assignment through seven checks, get concrete redesign moves for the exposed ones, and draft a student-facing GenAI policy you can paste into a syllabus.

Everything you type stays in this page. No account, no network, nothing saved anywhere but your screen.

Why this exists. MassBay's own English assessment reports found that while students widely use GenAI (about 48% for ideas, 38% for language, 42% for editing), 30% said it wasn't discussed in class, 60% not in the syllabus, and 75% not in the assignment instructions — and many feared being misread as dishonest. Silence is the gap. This tool closes it on both ends: the assignment and the syllabus.
