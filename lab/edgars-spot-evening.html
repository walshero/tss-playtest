Before the doors. Set the high-top from memory.

The direction

Open tight on one table. The room widens only when a regular walks in, so the player meets the cafe one person at a time and never sees the whole floor until it is full.

The room is a tabletop diorama: real tableware on the cover's red, shot from straight above, one soft light from the upper left. Hands only, no faces. The light tells the time: low sun through the window at five, dusk by seven-thirty, a lamp pooling over every table by nine.

Every usual is set down a beat before its guest arrives. That is the game's verb on the first screen: you remember, and the room answers.
