Gene read

Notes

How to play

  • Steer the falling piece with Left, Right and Turn. Drop sends it down fast.
  • Swipe a settled letter toward a neighbor to swap them, or tap one and then the other. A swap only sticks when it makes a read.
  • Three or more of one letter in a row or column makes a read. Four counts double.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys steer, Up turns, Space drops, P pauses.

What is real

  • Epigenetic marks change which genes a cell reads without changing the DNA letters.
  • Methyl groups on DNA (the gray caps) stop a gene from being read. Enzymes called DNMTs write them; TET enzymes begin taking them off.
  • DNA wraps around histone proteins like thread on a spool. Acetyl groups on histones loosen the wrap so the gene can be read; HDAC enzymes take them off and the wrap tightens.
  • Nearly every cell in your body carries the same DNA. Marks help make a skin cell different from a nerve cell.

Studio inventions

The falling pieces, swapping, the eraser and key as pieces, rows as spools, reads as runs of three, and signals on a timer are game rules, not biology.
